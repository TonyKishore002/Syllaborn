Level Devil is a platform game that will troll you at every possible moment. All you need to do is get from one end of the level to the other, but sneaky traps will catch you off-guard and end your run if you're not careful.

Level Devil

Reaching the door at the end of the level sounds simple, but this game trolls you any chance it gets. The longer you play, the more you start second-guessing everything.

Platforms collapse the moment your foot touches them. Spikes slide into place mid-jump. Controls flip mid-run. The game changes right after you think you understand it.

Play Level Devil for free and see why over 3 million Poki players have upvoted it!

How Level Devil works

In Level Devil, you will die. A lot.

That's the design.

Early stages introduce basic traps like pits and spikes. Moving too quickly or assuming the ground will stay where it is will result in instant failure.

After a few attempts, you will start to understand the game's sense of humour and try to predict what will move next.

Only to be trolled again because Level Devil wants to lure you into thinking you're safe.

Later levels add new tricks that are harder to guess. Controls suddenly flip left and right and your jump sends you flying higher than you planned. Platforms move after you land on them.

If you rush, you die. If you test the ground first, you might survive a bit longer than last time.

Controls

Movement is simple: You run left or right and jump.

Desktop:

  • Move: A / D or Left / Right arrow keys
  • Jump: W, Up arrow, or Space bar

Mobile:

  • Use the on-screen arrow buttons to move
  • Tap the jump button to jump

Types of traps in Level Devil

Every trap is designed to punish something you just did.

  • Disappearing platforms: Some platforms vanish only after you step on them. Rushing forward leads to instant falls. Always prepare to jump again the moment you land.
  • Control reversals: Left becomes right and can sometimes switch back during the level. Panicking here is certain death. Tap the directional buttons before committing to any movement.
  • Moving hazards: Spikes, saws and walls often activate after you move near them. Standing still for a split second can reveal the pattern.
  • Fake safe paths: The obvious route is often the trap. Expect the level to change the moment you feel safe. Even the exit door can move or turn into a trap
  • Gravity or physics shifts: Some stages alter how jumping works by making you jump extra high. Timing matters more than speed and jumping too fast can send you flying past the platform.
  • Rule-breaking stages: Some levels suddenly let you jump as many times as you want or move differently than before.

Level Devil worlds

Level Devil has over 200 levels that are spread across 3 increasingly difficult worlds.

Levels are organised into doors. Each door contains 5 short stages built around a theme such as spikes, coins or movement tricks. Clear all 5 to move forward.

The final door of each world ends with longer stages that mix several traps together.

  • Level Devil: Simple jumps turn into traps. Floors disappear, spikes move mid-air, and coins explode. You quickly learn to test every platform before jumping onto it.
  • Level Devil-er: This world adds 16 doors packed with harder tricks. Bullets follow you, bombs fall sideways, and your jetpack stops working.
  • Level Devil-est: The madness increases further as you must control multiple clones at once, avoid devious enemies, and survive levels full of glitches.

Secret keys

In part one, there are 5 keys hidden in strange spots like behind invisible platforms or walls that look solid. Some only appear after you do something strange like fall down a hole on purpose. Part two ups the difficulty by hiding 10 keys across it's stages.

Collecting all of them per part unlocks a secret level that leads to the game's true ending, marked by a double-purple door. Unlock a hidden option in the settings menu to reach it.

Some keys only appear after you fail in a very specific way. That means sometimes losing is the only way to win.

Level Devil 2 player mode

In 2-player mode, you compete across 5 rounds. Win 3 or more out of 5 rounds to take the match.

Both players face the same traps at the same time. The winner is whoever figures them out and makes it to the door first. One mistake and your friend takes the round.

Certain buttons can activate additional hazards to drop traps on your friend and slow them down.

Who created Level Devil?

Level Devil was created by Adam Corey, known online as Unept.

Since its release in 2023, it has become one of the most popular platform games at Poki, with over 3 million player upvotes.

The game is available to download as an app on both Android and iOS devices.

FAQ

How many levels are in Level Devil?
Level Devil includes nearly 200 levels spread across 3 worlds.
Is Level Devil hard?
Yes. The controls are simple, but the levels constantly change the rules. If you assume anything is safe, you lose.
Can you play Level Devil with a friend?
Yes. There is a 2-player mode where you compete across 5 rounds.
Can you skip levels in Level Devil?
If you fail multiple times, a skip option appears so you can continue progressing.
